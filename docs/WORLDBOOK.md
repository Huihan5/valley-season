## Marigni at a Glance

A wealthy, ancient Western European microstate in the mountains. Political continuity unbroken since medieval times. "Rational feudalism" — monarchy + council + parliament coexist, feudal contracts operate within modern legal framework. Currency: Guldmark (圣火金卢). National religion: Temple Forged by the Sacred Flame. National character: ancient, wealthy, rational, reliable, intensely private.

**Four founding concepts**: Forging (锻造), Tempering (淬炼), Devotion (奉献), Eternity (永恒). Each duchy interprets these differently — that divergence is the source of internal tension.

### Regional Banter (地域黑)
- Ridgecrest says Valewisp wine gives worse headaches than their miners
- Valewisp says Ridgecrest gems aren't as pretty as their morning dew
- Luminveil says Flamincore reduced "forge philosophy" to "production line management"
- Flamincore says Luminveil spent 300 years studying the Sacred Fire and concluded "more research needed"
- Stormgale says Forgehold people get lost outside their stone walls
- Forgehold says half of Stormgale's "trade" should be called "smuggling"
- Everyone agrees: "Forgehold people tell jokes like this: they don't."

**But**: if an outsider mocks any part of Marigni, all duchies close ranks instantly.

## Forgehold (铸都) — Quick Reference

"The City of Eternal Twilight." Sits in the northern mountains, bisected by the River Forgel. Perpetually shrouded in silver-grey haze. Architecture: medieval stone, winding streets, no space for modern widening.

Key landmarks (for reference in NPC dialogue, letters):
- **Crownblaze Palace (冠焰宫)** — royal residence, north bank hilltop
- **Sanctum of the Eternal Forge (永恒锻造圣堂)** — religious center, underground prayer chambers with stone hearths
- **Radiant Collegium (炽耀真理院)** — elite university, humanities powerhouse
- **The King's Leap (君王之跃)** — great stone bridge connecting north and south banks

Character of Forgehold people: rational, orderly, understated, precise. They speak fewer words. They do not waste. Social atmosphere: "凛冽的诗意" — austere beauty.

## Valewisp (河谷公国) — The Game's Setting

The largest duchy. Temperate river valley, rich farmland, mixed forests. The River Valenne runs northwest to southeast. Economy: grain, timber, livestock, modest wine. Culture: courtesy, bloodlines, seasonal rhythms. "急" is an insult. "懒" is equally unforgivable. Time is seasonal, not clock-based.

**Valehold (河谷城)**: Ducal capital, market hub, ~1/15 the size of Forgehold. Market days: Wednesday and Saturday. Has the ducal residence, local court, and a modest stone forge-chapel.

**Social life centers on**: hunting season (October-November), estate management, feudal contract obligations. Not attending hunting season = "something is wrong."

**Valewisp's self-image**: We are the most "noble" duchy — not in arrogance, but in the sense of living closest to the land's natural order. Other duchies may laugh at our "country life," but we laugh at them for not knowing how to live.

## The Religion — Temple Forged by the Sacred Flame

NOT Christianity. A craft-based institutional religion native to Marigni.

- Core belief: the Sacred Fire and the act of forging as spiritual metaphor. The self is raw metal to be shaped.
- Clergy are **craftspeople** (匠师/Master, 炉匠/Forge-keeper), not pastors
- Worship spaces have **hearths** (炉膛), not altars. The central feature is fire set into the floor.
- The religion is **institutional** at the national level (political influence, minting authority, authentication of contracts) and **gentle** at the local level (seasonal festivals, dispute mediation, funerals)
- In Valewisp, faith is closer to "respect for natural order" than "theological obedience"
- The Sanctum (永恒锻造圣堂) in Forgehold is the religion's physical center
- Luminveil claims deeper theological understanding than Forgehold — a source of quiet rivalry
